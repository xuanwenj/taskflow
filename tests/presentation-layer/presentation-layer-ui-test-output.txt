Summary: 7/8 passed.

Investigation of the T14 failure (measured in the browser after Run 1):
  - The original check combined three conditions in one message and required the title and
    action cells to have (almost) the same top. They are vertically centred, so their tops
    differed by 5-7 px. This part was a flaw in the check.
  - Looking at the mobile preview also showed a real layout defect: in each task card the
    edit and delete icons were left-aligned inside a wide column instead of sitting at the
    card's right edge, and their position varied from row to row.

Changes made after Run 1:
  - style.css: added "justify-self: end" to the actions cell in the mobile layout.
  - test-ui-structure.js: replaced the same-top check with a "shares a line" (vertical overlap)
    check, split the combined condition into separate messages, and added a check that the
    action icons are within 12 px of the right edge of the due date column.
